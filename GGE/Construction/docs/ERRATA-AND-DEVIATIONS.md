# Errata and deviations

## External sources

No verified erratum in an external source used by this release was identified in the scoped release review. This is not an audit of the external literature.

## Scope of the Lean statements

The fixed-period manuscript is not part of this release. The notes below describe what the exported Lean statements assert; they are not corrections to the manuscript or to the cited literature.

- The spectral arguments use the reflection and doubled-interval conventions of the fixed-period source. The certified spectral comparison is a determinant comparison; there is no additional Pfaffian identity in the release.
- The release does not identify the canonical Schur coordinates with Birkhoff coordinates.
- The Fourier density result is convergence in $L^1$ on a common Gaussian space. It does not assert total-variation convergence of the finite-plane laws.
- Bounds requiring $p>2$ retain the strict inequality. The conditional partition-function result retains its explicit premises.
- The all-charge lattice continuum limit, the thermodynamic limit, and the separate additive physical invariance result for the fourth charge are outside this release.
