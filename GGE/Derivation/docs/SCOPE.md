# Scope of the formalization

The statement about the nearby-weight lattice moment is conditional on the lattice hypotheses in the Lean declaration. Those hypotheses are not realized here. The result on the Birkhoff flow corresponding to E01 has only the scope specified in Lean. The determinant result corresponding to F01 uses a direct Hilbert–Schmidt, trace and regularized-determinant argument.

The excluded analytic and fixed-length results (including C-ANALYTIC, M4, U02 and U03) are not claimed. The all-regularity Comparator result applies to the three final statements listed in the README, not to every imported definition or auxiliary declaration. Its comparison statement is the approved Lean statement, not an independent formalization of the manuscript.
